Echinacea, also known as coneflower, is native to the Rocky Mountains and the United States. They are closely related to daisies; their flowers visually look similar, but the echinacea has pink petals.
Historically, the plant was used for respiratory tract infections by Native Americans. They also used it for snake bites and tooth pain. Echinacea is a good source of antioxidants and is known to help support the immune response. It is also used topically to help treat things like eczema and acne.
