Lemon Balm is a plant native to Southern-Central Europe, Iran, the Mediterranean, and Central Asia. It’s an herb in the mint family, and when the leaves are smelled, they have a light lemon scent. It can grow up to 39 inches tall, and the aforementioned leaves are soft and hairy with scalloped edges.
Lemon balm historically was coined the “elixir of life” and was valued for its mood-lifting and digestive properties. People currently use it for similar needs, as well as implementing it in topical creams for cold sores for relief.
