Eucalyptus typically grows on shrubs or trees native to Australia. The plant grows rapidly and can grow up to 300 feet tall. There are more than 660 different species of Eucalyptus; however, most of them are known for the distinct, greenish silver leaves.
Eucalyptus leaves contain Eucalyptus Oil. This has many antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antibacterial properties. It can also be used in dental care, with some studies citing it to help reduce plaque and gum bleeding. Historically, it was used by Indigenous Australians mainly for antiseptic uses or to treat colds.
