Chamomile is a plant that grows flowers that look strikingly similar to a daisy’s. It grows in sunny fields or any other areas with vegetation. Depending on the type of chamomile plant, it can grow anywhere from 1 ft to 3 ft tall. The leaves are thin, and alongside multiple flowers, a full plant can appear bushy.
Chamomile tea is a popular form; it can also be found in tablets, capsules, oils, and skin creams. It’s currently used to relieve anxiety and also for sleep benefits. Historically, it had also been used for things such as colds, sore throats, and nausea.
