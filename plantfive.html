Lavender is a flowering plant that is a part of the sage family, native to the Mediterranean region. The plant grows small, light purple flowers on tall, spikey leaves. The plant is known for its fragrance.
Taken orally, lavender oil could be beneficial for anxiety. People also cite lavender for improving sleep and relieving pain. Historically, Lavender had been used by Egyptians for embalming. It had also been used by Rome for its antiseptic and healing qualities. Even in the Middle Ages, Queen Elizabeth used lavender to help aid her frequent migraines.
