Dandelions are a hardy plant that can grow just about anywhere. They have vibrant yellow flowers with leaves that appear to be spikey. The plant can survive through competition with other weeds and droughts since they have a deep tap-root.
Dandelions although seen as a nuisance to most, are also deeply cherished medicinally. It is high in nutrients, higher than evne broccoli. It is also used for a lot of other ailments such as digesive health, cholesterol mangement, kidney support and even immune system support. It has been used for centuries to treat the same ailments, creating a rich history for the plant.
